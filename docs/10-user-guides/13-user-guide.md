# 13. User Guide (MVP)

## 13.1 Sign in
1. Open the application URL provided by the administrator.
2. Enter your assigned email and password.
3. Sign in.
4. If access is denied, contact the administrator; do not share credentials.

## 13.2 Register a product
1. Open Catalogue → Products.
2. Select Add Product.
3. Enter SKU, name, category, brand, cost and selling price.
4. Choose tracking mode: Serialized for IMEI/serial-tracked devices; Quantity for non-serialized stock.
5. Save and confirm the product appears in the catalogue.

## 13.3 Create a purchase and receive stock
1. Open Procurement → Suppliers and select/create supplier.
2. Create a purchase order and choose destination branch.
3. Add products, quantities and unit costs.
4. Submit/approve according to role permissions.
5. When goods arrive, open Receive Goods.
6. Enter received quantities and serial/IMEI values for serialized products.
7. Confirm receipt; review generated stock movement and branch balance.

## 13.4 Complete a POS sale
1. Open POS and confirm assigned branch.
2. Search and add products.
3. Select the exact serialized unit when applicable.
4. Add customer details if required.
5. Confirm payment method and amount.
6. Complete sale.
7. Display/print receipt and confirm inventory was updated.

## 13.5 Warranty lookup
1. Open Warranty → Lookup.
2. Search using receipt number or serial/IMEI.
3. Review customer, product, purchase date and warranty status.
4. If a claim is raised, record issue details and follow approval process.

## 13.6 Safe operating practices
- Never share user credentials.
- Do not bypass the application for stock adjustments.
- Record a reason for corrections.
- Do not delete completed sales; use approved return/refund workflow.
- Report suspicious activity or incorrect stock promptly.
